A nursery

Many of the fish, shrimp and crabs caught off this coast spend their early life among mangrove roots.

A buffer

Dense roots and trunks slow waves and hold mud in place before the sea reaches the villages behind them.

A carbon store

Mangroves bury carbon in the waterlogged mud beneath them, where it can stay locked away for a very long time.
