About us

Students, watching a coast.

CoastLens is a student project from FAST-NUCES Karachi, built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, “Dancing with the SARs”. We study one place, Keti Bandar in the Indus Delta, and try to say only what the satellites can support.

Our rules

What we say, and what we refuse to say.

We do say
  • How much area was mapped as mangrove inside one fixed box, and when.
  • What NISAR, Landsat and Sentinel actually measured, with dates and resolution.
  • Which places deserve a visit on the ground.
We do not say
  • Why a change happened. Every cause on this site is a hypothesis.
  • That a darker radar image means a dead forest.
  • Anything about fish catches, incomes, or the work of any organization.
Data and sources

Where every number comes from.

NISAR

NASA-ISRO L-band radar. L2 GCOV and GUNW provisional products from the ASF DAAC. Tracks 142 and 164, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026. Processed by CoastLens.

Radar

Global Mangrove Watch v3

Mangrove extent maps for 1996, 2007, 2010 and 2020 (Bunting et al., 2022). All area, loss and gain figures. An external product, not our own classification.

Extent

Landsat 8 · Sentinel-2 · Sentinel-1

Dry-season (Nov to Feb) cloud-masked composites via Google Earth Engine. Photos, greenness (NDVI) and C-band radar.

Optical + radar

Outside figures

Global area: Global Mangrove Watch v3. Indus Delta total: Ahmed et al., Ecological Indicators, 2025. Kharo Chan villages: The News on Sunday, as reported. These are not our measurements and cover different areas.
